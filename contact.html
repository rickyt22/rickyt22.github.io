Who you are

What to tell us, so the first reply is useful.

You are a practice

Helpful to include:

  • Your specialty, and the conditions you manage most
  • Roughly how many patients you would consider enrolling
  • Which EHR you use
  • What you want monitoring to change for your patients

You are a patient

Helpful to include:

  • Your name and the practice that enrolled you
  • What is happening — the device, a reading, or a call
  • The best number and time to reach you

For step-by-step help with the monitor itself, the blood pressure guide covers most questions and is available in English and Spanish.

Please do not send clinical details by email. Email is not a secure channel. Tell us that you need to discuss something clinical and we will arrange a safe way to do it. If you have a medical emergency, call 911.
